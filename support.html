Setup Guide

Follow these steps to enable Mizo Keyboard on your iPhone:

1

Open Settings

Go to your iPhone's Settings app

2

Navigate to Keyboard Settings

Tap General → Keyboard → Keyboards

3

Add New Keyboard

Tap "Add New Keyboard..." and select "Mizo Keyboard"

4

Enable Full Access (Optional)

Tap on Mizo Keyboard and enable "Allow Full Access" for GIF and sticker features. This is optional - basic typing works without it.

5

Start Using

Open any app with a text field, tap the globe 🌐 icon to switch to Mizo Keyboard

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I type the Ṭ character?

Long-press the T key on the keyboard. A popup will appear with Ṭ and other variants. Slide your finger to select the one you want.

How do I access accented vowels (á, é, í, etc.)?

Long-press any vowel key (A, E, I, O, U) to see all available accent marks and tone variants commonly used in Mizo.

Is my typing data safe?

Yes! Mizo Keyboard does not collect, store, or transmit your keystrokes. All data stays on your device. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

Why does the keyboard ask for "Full Access"?

Full Access is only needed for features that require internet access, like GIF search. Basic typing, autocorrect, and all core features work without Full Access. We recommend enabling it only if you want to use GIFs and stickers.

How do I access GIFs, Stickers, and Phrases?

Long-press the emoji button (😊) on the bottom row. A popup will appear with options for Stickers, GIF, Phrases, and Clipboard.

How does Text Expansion work?

Text expansion lets you create shortcuts. For example, typing "kle" can automatically expand to "Ka lawm e". You can manage your shortcuts in the main app settings.

The keyboard isn't showing up. What should I do?

Try these steps:

1. Make sure the keyboard is added in Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards

2. Restart your iPhone

3. If still not working, remove the keyboard and add it again

How can I suggest new words for the dictionary?

Email us at lalrin.sangsiama@gmail.com with word suggestions — we review and add new Mizo words in app updates.

Can I use swipe typing?

Yes! Swipe typing is enabled by default. Simply glide your finger from letter to letter without lifting, and the keyboard will predict your word.
