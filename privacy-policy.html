Mizo Keyboard

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 16, 2026

Our Commitment: Mizo Keyboard is offline-first. Your keystrokes are never recorded or transmitted. The keyboard works fully offline; a few clearly-labeled features are optional and only send data off your device when you explicitly turn them on and use them (voice typing, community GIF/sticker contributions, and — in the main app only — anonymous analytics/crash reporting). Each is described below.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mizo Keyboard ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS keyboard application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

By using Mizo Keyboard, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not do:

  • Keystrokes: We do not record, store, or transmit anything you type
  • Passwords: We have no access to your passwords or sensitive credentials
  • Personal Messages: Your texts, emails, and messages remain completely private
  • Location Data: We do not access or track your location
  • Contacts: We do not access your contact list
  • Browsing History: We have no access to your browsing activity

3. Information Stored Locally

Some features require storing data on your device. This data never leaves your device:

Feature Data Stored Purpose
Autocorrect Learning Word preferences Improve typing suggestions
Text Expansion Custom shortcuts Quick text insertion
Clipboard History Recent copied items Quick paste access
Keyboard Usage Stats Aggregated counters (e.g. words typed), on device Show you your own stats

Local Storage Only: The data in the table above is stored exclusively on your device (UserDefaults in a shared App Group) and never leaves it. The keyboard's own usage counters are aggregated on-device and are separate from the main app's optional analytics described in section 4.5.

4. Optional Features

These features are off or inert until you turn them on. Each is the only way data can leave your device.

4.1 Voice Typing (Opt-in, off by default)

Voice typing is disabled by default and the microphone key is hidden until you enable it and configure a speech-to-text (STT) endpoint in the main app's settings. When enabled and used:

  • Tapping the mic records short audio and uploads it to the STT endpoint you configured, over HTTPS, for transcription.
  • Requests carry an anonymous, per-install device identifier (used only to rate-limit abuse) — never your name, Apple ID, or contact info.
  • Audio is used to produce the transcription. It is retained for model training only if you separately opt in to "help improve Mizo speech recognition"; otherwise it is not kept for training.
  • The microphone is only ever active while you are holding/using voice typing.

4.2 GIFs & Stickers

Mizo Keyboard does not use GIPHY, Tenor, or any third-party GIF ad network, and no search query is sent to any such provider. GIFs and stickers come from:

  • A set bundled inside the app (fully offline), and
  • An optional community library synced through Apple iCloud (CloudKit). Browsing the community library fetches public records from iCloud; nothing about your search is shared with a third party.

4.3 Community Contributions (Opt-in)

You may voluntarily contribute a GIF to the shared community library. If you choose to:

  • Your submission is stored in a public Apple iCloud (CloudKit) database and becomes visible to other users of the app.
  • Only the content you submit plus an anonymous device identifier is stored — not your identity.
  • Contributing requires being signed in to iCloud and agreeing to the in-app Community Guidelines.
  • Do not submit private or personal images/text — public contributions are visible to everyone.
  • Reporting and blocking: you can report any community item (long-press → Report) or block a contributor (long-press → Block Creator). Reports store the reported item's ID, your anonymous device identifier, and a reason — nothing else. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours; email lalrin.sangsiama@gmail.com for takedown requests.

4.4 Clipboard History

If you enable clipboard history (requires Full Access), recently copied items are stored on your device only, capped to a small number of recent items, and never uploaded. Password-field copies are not saved.

4.5 Analytics & Crash Reporting — Main App Only (Optional)

The main Mizo Keyboard app (not the keyboard extension) may include Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand feature usage and diagnose crashes:

  • This runs only in the container app, never in the keyboard while you type.
  • It collects aggregated product-interaction events, performance data, and crash reports, not linked to your identity and not used to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Firebase is enabled only if the app is built with Google's configuration file; where present, it is governed by Google/Firebase's privacy terms.

5. Keyboard Permissions

iOS requires keyboard extensions to request "Full Access" for certain features. Here's what this means:

About Full Access: Even with Full Access enabled, Mizo Keyboard never records or transmits your keystrokes. Full Access is required by iOS for network features (community GIFs/stickers and voice typing) and for clipboard history. If you don't enable it, those features are simply unavailable and the rest of the keyboard works normally.

Permission Used For Required?
Full Access Clipboard history, community GIFs/stickers (network), voice typing Optional
Microphone Voice typing (only when enabled) Optional
Allow Haptics Key press feedback Optional

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures:

  • All local data uses iOS storage (UserDefaults with App Groups); the keyboard is offline by default.
  • Optional network features (voice typing) use HTTPS; community contributions use Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) infrastructure.
  • Off-device data carries only an anonymous per-install identifier, never your identity.
  • We follow iOS security best practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Mizo Keyboard is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we don't collect personal data from any users, there is no special risk for children.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: View your locally stored data in the app settings
  • Delete: Clear all local data by uninstalling the app or using the "Reset" option in settings
  • Opt-out: Disable optional features like analytics at any time
  • Export: Export your custom text expansions and settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Displaying a notice in the app for significant changes

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
